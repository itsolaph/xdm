XDM bundles snapshots of the following third-party lists. They are separate works, redistributed unmodified
apart from removing comments and rule types XDM does not use (scriptlets, CSS injection, HTML filtering).
"Update filter lists" in Settings replaces them with fresh copies from the same sources.
