YouTube downloads use NewPipeExtractor, which XDM includes as a library (unmodified).

NewPipeExtractor
  Source:  https://github.com/TeamNewPipe/NewPipeExtractor
  Authors: Team NewPipe and contributors
  License: GNU General Public License v3 or later (GPL-3.0.txt)
  Version: see gradle/libs.versions.toml ("newpipeExtractor")

It brings its own dependencies: nanojson (Team NewPipe fork, public domain / Unlicense), jsoup (MIT),
Protocol Buffers lite (BSD-3-Clause), Mozilla Rhino (MPL-2.0, see MPL-2.0.txt), JSR-305 annotations (BSD-3-Clause).

Because XDM contains a GPL-3.0 library, XDM as a whole has to be passed on under the GPL-3.0 terms.
